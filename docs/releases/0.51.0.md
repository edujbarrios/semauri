# Semauri 0.51.0 — Unicode text expressions and notebook inspection

Semauri 0.51.0 extends the statically typed, deterministic `.suri` language
and its Jupyter integration. The new expressions are pure, contextual and
supported by parsing, typed HIR, optimizer, runtime, formatting, highlighting
and regression tests. They require no additional capabilities or dependencies.

## New language PRs

| PR | Delivered change |
| --- | --- |
| [#264](https://github.com/edujbarrios/semauri/pull/264) | Contextual `uncapitalize of STRING`, preserving the remaining scalars |
| [#265](https://github.com/edujbarrios/semauri/pull/265) | Contextual `titlecase of STRING` for Unicode-whitespace-delimited words |

Non-string operands report `S351`. The names remain valid variables outside
the `<operator> of` context. Uppercase and lowercase expansions follow
Rust Unicode character casing rules. `titlecase` treats whitespace, not
punctuation, as the word delimiter and preserves original whitespace.

## Example

```text
assert (capitalize of "élève") is equal to "Élève".
assert (swapcase of "HeLLo") is equal to "hEllO".
assert (uncapitalize of "ÉTÉ") is equal to "éTÉ".
assert (titlecase of "hELLO wORLD") is equal to "Hello World".
print titlecase of "ßETA ÉTÉ".
```

Expected stdout:

```text
SSeta Été
```

Runnable regression-backed examples:
`examples/text_capitalize.suri`, `examples/text_swapcase.suri`,
`examples/text_uncapitalize.suri`, and `examples/text_titlecase.suri`.

## More read-only Jupyter commands

Notebook cells now support `%%tokens`, `%%ast`, `%%hir`,
`%%optimize`, `%%symbols`, `%%graph` and `%%fmt`, in addition
to prior execution/check/explain/effects/plan commands. `%%fmt` only
prints normalized source and never writes notebook cells or files.
Cells are isolated programs; bindings do not persist between cells.
